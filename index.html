Pima Indians Diabetes EDA

Exploratory analysis of a clinical dataset to find what separates women with and without diabetes.

  • 768 patients, 8 clinical variables
  • Patients with diabetes cluster at higher glucose levels
  • Strongest correlations: age–pregnancies (0.54) and BMI–skin thickness (0.53)

pandas · seaborn · descriptive statistics · data visualization

Music Recommendation System

Recommends the top 10 songs each user is most likely to play, after benchmarking six approaches.

  • 3,155 users, 563 songs, 117,876 interactions (filtered from 2 million)
  • Best model: tuned user-user collaborative filtering, F1 = 0.513 (precision 0.43, recall 0.64)
  • Content-based TF-IDF model to find songs similar to a given one

Surprise · collaborative filtering · matrix factorization · GridSearchCV · TF-IDF
