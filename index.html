About

I'm an electronics engineer with more than ten years across FPGA signal processing, chip verification and large-scale data analysis. My strength is combining high-performance software in Go and Python with RF digital signal processing, and I like owning a problem from the measurement to the tool that ships.

I led the technical work of Synopsys's Product Validation team in Chile and published research on radio-astronomy receivers in Astronomy & Astrophysics and SPIE. Today I want to bring that experience to RF, SDR and space systems.
