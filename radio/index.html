SDR Radio from Santiago

Notes from my software-defined radio station: an RTL-SDR Blog V4L dongle with a dipole antenna, on Windows, in Santiago, Chile. I write them to keep track of what I try: installing the drivers, scanning the FM band, recording Meteor weather satellites, and measuring ISDB-Tb digital TV.

Everything is written step by step, with the exact commands I used and what came out, including the attempts that did not work. Script names, options and console output are kept exactly as they appear in my tools, which use Spanish identifiers.

Guides

  • 1. Setup and first tests

    drivers, Zadig, SDR#, SDR++, rtl_test, antenna.

  • 2. Wideband sweeps with rtl_power

    an FM band example and how to avoid overload.

  • 3. Satellites

    predict passes, record automatically with a Windows task, then analyze and decode with SatDump.

  • 4. Digital TV

    map the UHF ISDB-Tb channels with rtl_power and canales_tv.py.

  • 5. ISDB-Tb recording for FPGA

    a validated IQ recording of channel 29, its OFDM parameters (mode, guard interval, CFO, SCO) and HDL testbench vectors.

  • 6. Home passes and the LNA

    a pass predictor for Viña del Mar checked against n2yo.com, and getting a 137 MHz filter + LNA working (power and gain).

  • 7. SSTV images from the ISS

    ARISS Series 34 on 437.550 MHz, a Python toolchain that removes the ±10 kHz Doppler shift and decodes Robot36, and two slides received from the Concón dunes.
